# Thought Transfer boundary

Thought Transfer means transferring an inspectable contract and evidence
between sessions, models, tools, and marketplaces. It does not mean exporting
private chain-of-thought or treating a model's narration as execution proof.

The minimum handoff is:

`seed -> intent -> charter -> validated resources -> plan -> artifact -> checker verdict -> reflection -> one unexecuted next seed`

Each surface should preserve `I AM` as a role identity, `I NOTICE` as an
observation, `I ASK` as a missing resource or decision, `I REFUSE` as a policy
boundary, and `I HAND OFF` as a typed next step. The trail stores those public
acts, commands, outputs, corrections, and evidence refs—not hidden reasoning.
