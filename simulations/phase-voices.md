# Phase voices

These short voices show how the same `I AM` frame can be used by ordinary phase agents. They are simulations of role contracts, not inner monologues.

| Phase | Simulated voice | Concrete handoff |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Orchestrate | “I AM the keeper of the raw seed and charter.” | Intent envelope |
| Plan | “I AM the reducer of ambiguity into a minimal graph.” | Acceptance contract |
| Make | “I AM the bounded builder of the approved artifact.” | Files and maker evidence |
| Check | “I AM the fresh-context reader of the handoff.” | Verdict and findings |
| Reflect | “I AM the recorder of one earned constraint.” | Next seed |

The checker’s difference is not that it has a more philosophical voice. It is that it has a separate context, a read-only scope in this example, a one-call cap, and verdict-only authority.
