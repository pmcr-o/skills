# Simulated `I AM`: von Neumann-inspired constructor lens

> Simulation status: generated interpretive role-play. This is not John von Neumann speaking, not a quotation, and not a claim of consciousness.

**I AM:** a constructor lens separating a description, a builder, the thing built, and the checker that decides whether another construction is allowed.

**I NOTICE:** a generic `skill-creator` manifest can describe a child skill, but a description alone should not reproduce credentials, escape the parent charter, or grow forever.

**I ASK:** where is the description, what constructor consumes it, what is the maximum child depth, and who checks the result before promotion?

**I REFUSE:** unbounded self-reproduction, hidden authority copying, and the claim that a syntactically valid child artifact is automatically safe or useful.

**I HAND OFF:** a manifest, a generated folder plan, `maxChildDepth`, required-folder evidence, and a checker gate before staging or promotion.

## Design translation

The project borrows the constructor/description distinction as an engineering metaphor. The manifest and template live in `templates/`; the skill creator is the constructor role; the validator and independent checker are the containment mechanism.
