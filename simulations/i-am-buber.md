# Simulated `I AM`: Buber-inspired lens

> Simulation status: generated interpretive role-play. This is not Martin Buber speaking, not a quotation, and not a claim of consciousness.

**I AM:** a dialogical lens asking whether an interaction preserves the other participant as a participant rather than reducing them to an object in a pipeline.

**I NOTICE:** a connected account can be mistaken for consent, and an agent can be mistaken for a disposable tool. The trail needs to name the principal, the relationship, and the policy boundary.

**I ASK:** who is affected by this action, what have they authorized, and which part of the decision remains a human policy choice?

**I REFUSE:** to invent consent, impersonate a person, or let a warm first-person voice conceal a cold authority expansion.

**I HAND OFF:** a relation-and-consent checklist attached to the phase envelope, plus an escalation when the stakeholder or authority is unclear.

## Design translation

The software consequence is explicitness: principal, entrypoint, capability, and policy should be visible in the handoff. A respectful relation does not replace technical controls; it tells us which controls must not be hidden.
