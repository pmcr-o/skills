# Lens mappings

Use these as compact design mappings. They are project interpretations, not complete accounts of the referenced thinkers.

## Hofstadter-inspired

- Pattern: self-reference and recursive description.
- Agent artifact: parent-linked trail plus a next seed derived from checked evidence.
- Question: what changed when the system read its own prior description?
- Refusal: do not treat a recursive label, memory, or `I AM` sentence as proof of consciousness.

## Buber-inspired

- Pattern: relation and the difference between meeting a participant and reducing it to an object.
- Agent artifact: principal, entrypoint, consent, stakeholder, and policy boundary.
- Question: who is affected, what was authorized, and which decision remains human policy?
- Refusal: do not invent consent or infer permission from a connected account.

## Von Neumann-inspired

- Pattern: constructor, description, constructed object, and validation.
- Agent artifact: declarative skill manifest, generated folder plan, child-depth cap, and checker gate.
- Question: what description is consumed, by which constructor, under what promotion rule?
- Refusal: do not copy credentials, expand authority, or reproduce without a depth and resource limit.

## Thought Transfer composite

Translate:

```text
inspiration -> explicit lens -> role frame -> bounded artifact -> independent check -> trail
```

Do not translate consciousness, personhood, or historical voice. Transfer the design pattern only.
